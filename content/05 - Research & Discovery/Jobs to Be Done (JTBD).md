# Jobs to Be Done (JTBD)

## Definition
Jobs to Be Done reframes user research around the "job" a customer is trying to get done, rather than the product they currently use or the demographic they belong to. The classic formulation: "when [situation], I want to [motivation], so I can [expected outcome]." People don't buy products, they "hire" them to make progress on a job.

## Why It Matters for PMs
JTBD keeps discovery focused on durable customer motivations instead of shifting feature requests or personas that can mislabel who actually buys and uses a product. It's especially useful for spotting indirect competitors — a spreadsheet and a project management tool can compete for the same "job" even though they look nothing alike.
