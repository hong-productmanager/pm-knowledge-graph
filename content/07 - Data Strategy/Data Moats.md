# Data Moats

## Definition
A data moat is a durable competitive advantage built from proprietary data that's hard for competitors to replicate — because it's expensive to collect, improves with scale (more users generate more data that improves the product, attracting more users), or is exclusive by contract or regulation.

## Why It Matters for PMs
Not all data creates a moat — a moat requires the data loop to actually improve the product in a way users notice and competitors can't copy quickly. PMs need to distinguish real data moats from "we have a lot of data" (which may be commoditized, low-quality, or irrelevant to the specific job the product does), since chasing a fake moat wastes roadmap capacity.
