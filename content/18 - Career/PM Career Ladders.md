# PM Career Ladders

## Definition
A PM career ladder defines the levels a product manager can progress through (e.g., APM → PM → Senior PM → Group/Principal PM → Director → VP) along with the scope, expected impact, and behaviors that distinguish each level — usually spanning execution, influence, strategy, and people/organizational leadership as axes.

## Why It Matters for PMs
Without a clear ladder, "am I ready for the next level" becomes a guessing game driven by tenure or self-perception rather than evidence. Understanding your company's actual ladder (not just the generic industry version) tells you which of your current projects double as promotion evidence, and where the gap is between your current scope and the next level's expectations.
