# RICE Scoring

## Definition
RICE is a prioritization framework that scores each candidate initiative on **R**each (how many users it affects in a period), **I**mpact (how much it moves the goal per user), **C**onfidence (how sure you are about the reach/impact estimates), and **E**ffort (person-time required). The score is `(Reach × Impact × Confidence) / Effort` — higher scores should be prioritized first.

## Why It Matters for PMs
RICE forces prioritization debates to surface their actual assumptions (a stakeholder who insists a feature is "high impact" now has to put a number on it) and makes tradeoffs comparable across very different types of work. It's not perfect — the inputs are still estimates — but it beats prioritizing by whoever argues loudest or most recently.
