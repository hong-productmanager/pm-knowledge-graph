# Kano Model

## Definition
The Kano Model classifies features by how they affect satisfaction: **basic** (expected — absence causes dissatisfaction, presence doesn't delight), **performance** (more is better, satisfaction scales linearly with quality), and **delighters** (unexpected — absence isn't noticed, presence creates disproportionate satisfaction). Features also tend to migrate from delighter to basic over time as users' expectations rise.

## Why It Matters for PMs
It's a corrective to prioritizing purely by "impact score": a basic feature can score low on excitement but be non-negotiable table stakes, while chasing delighters before the basics are solid produces a flashy product that still frustrates users on fundamentals. It also explains why yesterday's delighter (e.g., free shipping) is today's basic expectation.
