# North Star Metric

## Definition
A North Star Metric (NSM) is the single measure that best captures the core value a product delivers to customers, chosen so that if it goes up, the business is healthier as a result — not just growing a vanity number. It sits above individual team KPIs and gives every function a shared definition of "winning."

## Why It Matters for PMs
Without a shared NSM, teams optimize locally (engagement for growth, uptime for infra, deal count for sales) and can pull the product in conflicting directions. A well-chosen NSM lets a PM justify prioritization calls ("this ships because it moves the NSM") instead of relying on opinion, and it's the anchor a roadmap should trace back to.
