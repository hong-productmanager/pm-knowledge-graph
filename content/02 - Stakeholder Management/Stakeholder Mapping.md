# Stakeholder Mapping

## Definition
Stakeholder mapping is the practice of identifying everyone with influence over or interest in a product decision, then plotting them (commonly on a power/interest grid) to decide how much and what kind of engagement each one needs.

## Why It Matters for PMs
PMs sit at the intersection of engineering, design, sales, compliance, and leadership. Without a deliberate map, it's easy to under-communicate with a high-power stakeholder (and get blindsided later) or over-invest time in someone with low influence over the outcome. Mapping turns "who do I need to talk to" from a gut feeling into a repeatable exercise.
