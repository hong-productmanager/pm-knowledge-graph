# One-Pager / Product Brief

## Definition
A one-pager (or product brief) is a single-page artifact that states a problem, proposed direction, target outcome, and rough scope — deliberately shorter and less detailed than a PRD. It's meant to get quick alignment or a go/no-go decision before investing in the full detailed spec.

## Why It Matters for PMs
Writing a full PRD for an idea that leadership might reject wastes real effort; a one-pager lets a PM get directional buy-in cheaply, and forces enough clarity of thought to reveal whether an idea is actually well-formed before it's over-invested in. It's also a useful artifact for stakeholders who won't read a 6-page PRD but will read one page.
