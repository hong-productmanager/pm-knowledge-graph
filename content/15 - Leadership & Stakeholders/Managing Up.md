# Managing Up

## Definition
Managing up is the practice of deliberately working with your manager or executive stakeholders to align expectations, surface risks early, and make their job of supporting you easier — rather than treating the relationship as one-directional (they assign, you deliver).

## Why It Matters for PMs
PMs rarely have direct authority over the teams they depend on, so influence upward and sideways is often the actual mechanism that gets things prioritized, funded, or unblocked. A PM who manages up well gives their leadership no-surprises updates, brings decisions with a recommendation rather than an open-ended question, and knows which risks need escalating versus absorbing.
