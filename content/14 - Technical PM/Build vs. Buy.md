# Build vs. Buy

## Definition
Build vs. Buy is the decision of whether to develop a capability in-house or acquire it via a vendor, open-source library, or API. The decision usually weighs differentiation (is this core to your competitive advantage?), total cost of ownership, time-to-market, and long-term maintenance burden against the upfront cost and integration risk of a third party.

## Why It Matters for PMs
Technical PMs are frequently the ones framing this tradeoff for leadership — and the common failure mode is treating it as purely a cost comparison, when the real question is usually "is this the thing our product should be differentiated on?" Building commodity infrastructure wastes engineering time that should go toward the product's actual moat; buying your core differentiator hands it to a vendor.
