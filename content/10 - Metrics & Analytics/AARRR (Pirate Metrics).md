# AARRR (Pirate Metrics)

## Definition
AARRR breaks the user lifecycle into five stages to track and optimize: **A**cquisition (how users find you), **A**ctivation (their first good experience), **R**etention (do they come back), **R**eferral (do they bring others), and **R**evenue (do they pay). The name comes from the stage initials sounding like a pirate's "aarrr."

## Why It Matters for PMs
The framework's real value is diagnostic: it stops teams from throwing all their effort at acquisition when the actual leak is activation (users sign up but never reach the "aha moment") or retention (users churn after one use). PMs can use it to figure out which stage of the funnel is the actual bottleneck before proposing a fix.
