# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## Definition
A PRD is a written artifact that defines what a team is building and why: the problem, target user, success metrics, scope (explicitly including what's out of scope), and functional requirements — written at a level of detail that lets engineering and design start work without repeatedly asking "wait, what should happen when...?"

## Why It Matters for PMs
A PRD is a forcing function: writing one surfaces the questions you haven't answered yet, before they become expensive mid-sprint surprises. It also becomes the shared source of truth stakeholders can react to asynchronously, instead of requirements living only in the PM's head or scattered across Slack threads.
