# Product-Led Growth (PLG)

## Definition
Product-Led Growth is a go-to-market motion where the product itself — not a sales team — is the primary driver of acquisition, conversion, and expansion. Users typically self-serve into a free tier or trial, experience value quickly, and convert to paid without ever talking to a salesperson.

## Why It Matters for PMs
In a PLG motion, the product roadmap effectively *is* the GTM strategy — onboarding, activation, and in-product upgrade prompts carry weight that used to sit with sales and marketing. PMs need to own metrics (time-to-value, activation rate, self-serve conversion) that in a sales-led company would belong to a different team entirely.
