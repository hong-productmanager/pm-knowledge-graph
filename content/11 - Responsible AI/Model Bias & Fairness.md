# Model Bias & Fairness

## Definition
Model bias is systematic, unfair skew in a model's outputs across groups of users — often inherited from imbalanced or historically-biased training data, but sometimes introduced by the objective function itself (optimizing for an outcome that correlates with a protected characteristic). Fairness work is the practice of measuring, bounding, and mitigating that skew.

## Why It Matters for PMs
A biased model isn't just an ethical problem — it's a product and legal risk: it can produce outcomes that are unusable or harmful for a subset of your users, and increasingly it's a regulatory requirement (e.g., the EU AI Act) to be able to demonstrate you measured and mitigated it. PMs shipping AI features need to know what fairness metrics apply to their use case and who signs off before launch.
